Find my phone — certification notes (English, paste into Partner Center)

PRODUCT PURPOSE (policy 1.1.2)
This is NOT a phone tracker. It is a single-purpose Microsoft Edge toolbar shortcut that opens ONLY:
• https://www.google.com/android/find/ (Google Find My Device, Android)
• https://www.icloud.com/find/ (Apple iCloud Find, iPhone)
Ring, locate, lock, and erase happen only after the user signs in on Google or Apple. The publisher provides no maps, no location backend, and no accounts.

UI CLARITY (v1.1.5+)
The floating panel shows a blue information banner stating the extension does not track the phone and only opens those official URLs. Buttons are labeled as shortcuts to the official Google or Apple site. We do not supply Google/Apple test accounts; reviewers may stop after confirming the correct official page opens, or use their own accounts.

HOW TO TEST
1) Install the package and pin the extension if needed.
2) Click the toolbar icon. A floating panel opens with the disclaimer banner, Android / iPhone buttons, and language (English / Spanish).
3) Click Android or iPhone. A browser tab opens with the official locator URL (panel stays available). Sign in with your own Google or Apple account if you want to verify ring/locate flows.
4) Optional: enable "Remember my platform" and use "Open official site" on a later visit.

PERMISSIONS
• storage — UI language and optional "remember platform" only (chrome.storage.local).
• windows — Opens/focuses the floating panel window (chrome.windows).

DATA / PRIVACY
No publisher backend, no analytics. The extension does not read page content or harvest credentials.

STORE LISTING
Description and screenshots should match the panel: shortcut to official vendor pages only, not a third-party tracker.
